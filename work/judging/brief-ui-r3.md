## Stage 1 (must ship): the proof map is the first screen of every pull request
Replace the prose Brief with one composed screen whose centre is a **proof map of the whole change**:

- **Every changed line of the pull request is one mark**, grouped by file (file path as the row label, long lists of near-identical files collapse into a counted group you can open), in the order of the diff. A mark's colour says who stands behind that line:
  - **proven by a run** (the unit is `ask: 'none'` because executed evidence covers it: a test that fails without the change and passes with it, a snapshot check, a mechanical-edit check): the calm colour, the large mass of the picture;
  - **skim** (`ask: 'skim'`): a middle state;
  - **yours to read** (`ask: 'read'`): the one loud colour on the screen. It must pop out of 1,300 quiet marks at arm's length.
  - findings pinned as a small marker on the line they cite; a reproduced finding (a failing test exists) is distinguishable from a reasoned one.
- **One sentence in large type above it, computed, never hard-coded:** e.g. "49 of 1,392 changed lines are yours." with the counts per colour as the legend (legend = the three numbers, tabular figures). When the reading plan is void (`fullReadReason(d)` returns a reason: no requirement, the reviewer missed the bugs planted in this change, nothing ran, the area is on probation, a spot check) the sentence is "All 1,043 lines are yours." with the reason in one line, every mark turns to "yours", and the plan that would have applied is shown only as a faint outline or secondary legend. Never colour a line "proven" when the gate will ask the person to read it. The numbers on this screen must equal what the inbox and the sign-off statement say (the server computes `readLines`; `shared/gate.ts` is the single definition).
- **It is interactive, not a picture:** hover or arrow-key over a group of marks shows the actual code lines and, for proven lines, the proof in one line ("fails on base, passes on head: `SnapshotTests.Resolver` · E7", with the evidence id); click or Enter opens that unit with its real diff (the existing unit chapter and `DiffViewer`). Keyboard: the existing `N`/Enter "next thing that needs you" path still walks decisions, findings, units to read, then the signature; the map is where it starts and where it returns, and marks change state (read, judged) as the person works, so the map is also the progress view.
- **A right rail that replaces the prose**, dense and iconised: the requirement and its acceptance criteria as pass/fail/unproven chips (AC1..ACn, each opening its evidence); the decisions the AI refused to make as compact option cards (count, "no option recommended"); findings by severity with a "reproduced" mark; the reviewer's self-test as one line of numbers ("planted bugs caught 5 of 6 · second model read 1,343 skipped lines: 0 issues"); "what nobody checked" as a count that opens the list; then the **signature block**: the named person, what is left ("3 things left"), disabled until the gate is open.
- **The merge box:** under the signature block show GitHub's side as GitHub shows it: the required check `countersign/sign-off`, "Merging is blocked" with a grey, disabled "Merge pull request" button until a named person signs, green after. `OnGitHub.tsx` already loads what GitHub itself reports (`/api/dossiers/<slug>/github`); on this throwaway copy nothing is posted to GitHub, so say that in one quiet line instead of faking a green check ("This demo copy does not post to GitHub. On the connected instance the check turns green under your name."). Do not imitate GitHub's logo or pretend to be github.com; a plain, honest box in our own style.
- Cut the left navigation down: titles must not truncate into "Is it enough that the resolv…"; prefer short ids with an icon for state (decision open/answered, finding unjudged/real/not real, unit unread/read) and the full title on the stage. The optional "Evidence" chapters stay reachable but quiet.
- Three stacked control bars at the bottom compete (N left / Next; Back / Continue; Ask). One primary action at a time.

Keep everything true: you change presentation, never what a claim or number says. Text a human typed is shown exactly as typed.
